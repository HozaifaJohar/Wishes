# Delete your Wishes Beauty account

You can delete your Wishes Beauty account at any time.

## Delete it from the app

1. Open the Wishes app and sign in.
2. Go to **My account → Personal information**.
3. Tap **Delete account**, then confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Can't access the app?

Contact us from the phone number registered on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll confirm once it's done.

- **Email:** wishesbeauty963@gmail.com
- **Phone / WhatsApp:** <span dir="ltr">+963 991 146 777</span>

You can also contact us if you have any issue or question about deleting your account.

## What happens to your data

- **Deleted:** your account, name, phone number, saved addresses, wishlist and loyalty points.
- **Kept:** records of past orders, only for as long as the law requires (for example, tax and accounting records). They are not used for any other purpose.

After deletion, you can register again with the same phone number.
